I make the Tabi Pocket apps for visitors to Japan. I call every restaurant myself, in Japanese, and I tell you honestly when a place can’t be booked.

Questions

What if the restaurant is full?

We try your next choice (up to 3 restaurants and 2 date/time options). If none work, we refund the full $19 — you don’t need to ask.

How fast will I hear back?

Within 24 hours, Japan time. Restaurants are often closed on one weekday, so a few bookings take a little longer — we’ll tell you.

What if I need to cancel?

Email us at least 3 days before and we’ll call the restaurant. Many restaurants charge a fee for late cancellations or no-shows (often 50–100% of the meal), which they may ask you to pay. The $19 is not refunded once your table is booked.

Do you add a deposit or markup to the meal?

No. You pay the restaurant directly for what you eat. Our only charge is the $19 booking fee.

Can you book omakase sushi?

Often, yes — if the restaurant takes phone bookings. A few top places only accept bookings through their own system or from regulars; we’ll tell you honestly if that’s the case and refund you.
